01 · About

Two disciplines, one engineering mindset

How a STEM education and years of hands-on training turned into practical embedded and web engineering skills.

I'm a STEM student who spends most of my time where hardware and software meet — wiring up sensors, reading datasheets, and then building the interface or dashboard that makes the data useful.

My technical foundation was built at STEM Schools, where I worked on practical engineering, embedded systems, electronics, programming, and scientific projects rather than purely theoretical coursework.

Alongside that, I specialized for three years in Embedded Systems & Robotics through the Digital Egypt Cubs Initiative — a sustained, practical training track covering microcontrollers, sensors, electronics, and prototyping.

That combination is why I describe myself with two directions instead of one: Embedded Systems & Robotics for the physical, sensor-driven side of engineering, and Web Development for the interfaces, dashboards, and platforms that sit on top of it.

Embedded Systems

Microcontrollers, sensors, and firmware that read the physical world reliably.

Robotics & Automation

Turning sensor input into control logic — motors, actuators, and automated behavior.

IoT & Connectivity

Getting embedded devices onto the network so their data is actually usable.

Web Development

Frontend and backend work that turns raw project data into something people can use.
